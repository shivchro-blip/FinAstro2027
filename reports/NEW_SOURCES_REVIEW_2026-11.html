New sources review: Tajika Neelakanthi, Hayanaratna, Brihat Samhita (Iyer 1884)

Made 27 Nov 2026. It covers the three PDFs added to Reference_materials/Others/ on 27 Nov 2026 (PR #58). All three are Vedic, so they are in scope.

Every rule below is listed as the source states it, with a page reference. Nothing here is implemented or tested yet. The user picks which rules move into the project, and each one then gets the §6 backtest before it is used.

How they relate:

  • Hayanaratna quotes Nilakantha's Varsha Tantra ("Varṣatantra") throughout. The annual-chart rules of the two Tajika books are therefore largely the same text, and are listed once (Section 4).
  • The Iyer Brihat Samhita is the same work as the Sastri 1946 translation already in the project. It is used here to find chapters the earlier review did not cover, and to check readings.

4. Tajika annual and monthly chart rules (Hayanaratna and Nilakanthi Varsha Tantra)

These are written for a person's annual return (varsha pravesha) and monthly return. The Solar return method already treats the NYSE as a native, with an annual and monthly chart for its foundation chart, so these rules can be read the same way. That is still an application the Tajika sources do not make themselves, so each one is labelled.

IDRule as statedSourceUse
T1The annual return is the moment the Sun regains its sidereal natal longitude. The monthly return is the Sun reaching the same degree in each following sidereal sign. Lunisolar and tropical years are rejected.Hayanaratna 1.6 and 8.1–8.3 (pp.111–127, 885–923)A source for the method choice: supports Model B (sidereal monthly) in solar_return_engine.py over Model A (tropical)
T2Calendar at the annual return: Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday are excellent; Tuesday, Sunday and Saturday "bring loss and fear to the year". Nakshatras: Ashvini, Mrigashira, Hasta, Pushya, Punarvasu, Svati and Revati are auspicious. Bharani, Magha, Chitra, Vishakha, Shatabhisha, Dhanishta and Ashlesha are "much condemned". The remaining nakshatras are middling. Nanda/Bhadra/Jaya/Purna tithis are good, except tithis 1 and 12; Rikta tithis are not.Hayanaratna 1.7, from Trailokyaprakasha 193–197 (p.129)NYSE annual return (once a year)
T3Muntha: the natal ascendant advanced one sign per year. In the annual chart: 9th, 10th or 11th gives authority; 1st, 2nd, 3rd or 5th gives "wealth by enterprise"; 4th, 6th, 7th, 8th or 12th is not wanted, and the 7th, 8th and 12th bring loss of wealth. Muntha with benefics or its own lord is good, with malefics bad. Muntha in the "mouth" of Rahu (degrees Rahu has yet to cover) gives wealth; in its "tail" (7th from it) loss.Hayanaratna 5.1–5.5 (pp.423–449); Nilakanthi Muntha chapter (pp.146–153)NYSE annual chart
T4Ruler of the year: of five candidates (lords of the natal ascendant, the annual ascendant and the Muntha; the triplicity lord; and the lord of the Sun's sign by day or the Moon's by night), the strongest one aspecting the annual ascendant. A strong benefic ruler in an angle, trine or the 11th gives a good year; a weak one in the 6th, 8th or 12th an evil year. Combust or retrograde in both charts: "loss of wealth in every undertaking". A strong Jupiter as ruler: great gain of wealth; Venus or Mercury the same.Hayanaratna 5.8–5.11 (pp.457–521, 583); Nilakanthi (pp.137–145)NYSE annual chart
T52nd house (gold, silver, grains, buying and selling). Jupiter as 2nd lord in the 2nd in the annual chart, applying to the ascendant lord: gain all year; separating (isarapha) from a malefic: loss. A mutthashila (applying aspect) between the 2nd lord and the ascendant lord: easy gain. A corrupt 2nd lord and wealth-giver: loss.Hayanaratna 6.3 (pp.581–597); Nilakanthi 2nd-house verses (pp.65, 166)NYSE annual chart
T611th house (gains, buying). A strong 11th lord in itthasala with the ascendant lord or the ruler of the year: wealth. The ruler of the year in the 11th with benefics: gain from business. Mercury as ruler of the year in the 2nd or 11th, aspected by benefics: gain from trade.Hayanaratna 6.12 (pp.721–729); Nilakanthi 11th house v.1–2 (p.188)NYSE annual chart
T7Sahams (sensitive points), all used as "with benefics and their ruler: gain; with malefics: loss". Wealth saham: 2nd cusp − 2nd lord + ascendant. Trade (vanijya) saham: Moon − Mercury + ascendant. Business (vyapara) saham: Mars − Mercury + ascendant. Fortune saham: by day ascendant + Moon − Sun, at night reversed; "when Fortune is strong there is the means to wealth".Hayanaratna 4.1–4.6 (pp.355–421); Nilakanthi saham verses 16 and 20 (pp.112, 114)NYSE annual chart (and monthly)
T8Monthly chart: the ruler of the month is chosen like the ruler of the year, from the monthly chart. Navamsha rule: if the navamsha lord of the ascendant lord is friendly (by aspect or conjunction) with the navamsha lord of the 2nd house, "riches in the house"; inimical, fallen or combust means loss of wealth. The same with the 11th means "gain through business". The rulers of the ascendant, month and year in the 6th, 8th or 12th with malefics mean a bad month; strong in an angle, trine or the 11th mean gain.Hayanaratna 8.4 (pp.923–935), from Varsha Tantra 18.3–13, Vamana and Tajikasara 362–363NYSE monthly charts: the same frame as the Solar return method, with many more samples than the annual rules
T9Planets in the houses of the monthly chart: for example, the Sun in its own, exaltation or friend's sign in the 2nd gives acquisition of wealth, elsewhere in the 2nd loss of wealth. The Sun in the 11th gives gain; in the 12th loss. The Moon in the 2nd aspected by benefics gives property.Hayanaratna 8.5–8.6 (pp.937–949, Jirnatajika)NYSE monthly charts
T10Substance rule: "there will be gain of the substance of a planet that is strong in the year and loss of the substance of a planet that is weak". Silver belongs to Venus and the Moon; gold to Mercury, gold with gems to Jupiter.Hayanaratna 1.5 (pp.107–109), from Trailokyaprakasha 26–33Gold and silver strength from the annual chart. 6. Suggested order for moving rules into the project

  1. B12 (BS4 retest with an eclipse in the Leo month). It corrects a rule already used. It is cheap to test, and it bears on the sell window opening 16 Dec 2026.
  2. B1 (Moon north or south of Mercury, Jupiter and Venus) and B2 (Mercury's reappearance as volatility). Daily events with many samples; the full §6 backtest applies directly.
  3. B4, B6 and B8: planet-in-nakshatra and lord-of-day/month rules (general, many samples).
  4. T8 and T9 (monthly-chart rules) on the NYSE monthly returns: Model B frame, about 300 months since 2000, more since 1928.
  5. N1–N5, T2–T7: annual rules. Only one sample a year (about 98 since 1928 for the S&P), so expect wide error bars.
  6. Not proposed:
  7. Horary rules (N7, N8, and the house-by-house questions in Nilakanthi pp.217–255): they need a chart-time choice the source does not make.
  8. Crop-direction rules (N9).
  9. Mudda dashas (T11): dashas are parked.
  10. Colour and portent rules from Brihat Samhita Part II: not computable.

7. Conflicts to keep visible

  • The metal for gold:
  • Brihat Samhita 16.4 and Nilakanthi Samjna 69 give it to the Sun.
  • Hayanaratna (Trailokyaprakasha) and Nilakanthi Samjna 72 give it to Mercury.
  • The project's current convention (Sun → gold, from the Kindle Ch.15 note) stays until the user decides.
  • The metal for silver: the Moon (Kindle Ch.15), Venus and the Moon (Hayanaratna), and Venus for silver mines (Brihat Samhita 16.27).
  • Grain prices with the Sun in Sagittarius, Capricorn or Aquarius: Nilakanthi says benefics make grain cheap and malefics dear. Brihat Samhita says benefics make it moderate or rising, and malefics make it fluctuate or fall.
  • The ascendant lord joined with the 2nd lord:
  • Varsha Tantra (and Tejahsimha's applying-aspect version): good for wealth.
  • Vamana: "no happiness from wealth".
  • Hayanaratna 6.3 (p.583) records both views.
