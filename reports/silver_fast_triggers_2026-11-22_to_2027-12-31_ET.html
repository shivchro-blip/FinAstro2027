Silver: narrowing the watch windows with fast planets, Sun 22 Nov 2026 – Fri 31 Dec 2027

Slow-planet conditions set the window; does a Moon, Mercury, Venus, Sun or Mars trigger pick the day? Tested on silver (±3.5% days) 2000–2026: 4993 trading days fell inside a watch window (445 big days, 8.9%) against 1534 days outside (101 big, 6.6%). For each fast factor on the day, the big days inside windows are compared with the number expected from the same years' window days; it passes if lift > 1 with p < 0.10 and lift > 1 in both 2000–2013 and 2013–2026. 3 of 46 factors passed. With the returns shuffled within each year (100 times, so no link to the sky), 2.5 factors pass on average (90th percentile 5, maximum 7); none survives the false-discovery-rate correction, so the narrowed dates are best guesses inside the windows, not calls. The “outside” column shows the same factor's lift on days outside any window: a trigger that only works inside windows is what the slow-sets-fast-fires idea predicts.
