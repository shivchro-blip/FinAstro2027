All layers together: a probability for every day, 2026-09-24 – 2026-11-23

Each layer puts the day in a state (for example Tara bala good / bad / janma). A state's weight is how often the market rose in it before the scored period, shrunk toward the base rate (200 pseudo-days). The layers are joined on log-odds (naive Bayes): start at the base rate and let each layer move it. Skill = how much better the probabilities are than always quoting the base rate (Brier score; 0% = no better, negative = worse). Calls: up when the probability is 2+ points above the base rate, down when 2+ below. Kept layers improved the forecast of that target on their own over 2013–2026 (fitted on 2000–2012), chosen before looking at the 60 days.
