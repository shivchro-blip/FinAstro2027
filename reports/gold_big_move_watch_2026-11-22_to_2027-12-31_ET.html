Gold big-move watch, Sun 22 Nov 2026 – Fri 31 Dec 2027 (US Eastern)

Dates when the factors that were over-represented on gold's ±2% days in 2000–2026 (gold_big_moves_2000-2026_ET.html) come round again. On a normal day the chance of a ±2% close was 7.3%; the last column of the first table gives the historical rate when each lead was present (not adjusted for year, so it flatters factors active in volatile years).

How much to trust it. No lead passed the false-discovery-rate correction (lowest q 0.36). When leads were picked on 2000–2013 only, 14 of 23 stayed above 1 on 2013–2026, but taken together they gave 388 big days against 379.7 expected (×1.02): no edge out of sample. Read the dates below as times to expect volatility to be possible, not as calls. Green rows held in both halves and use Vedic grahas only; amber rows held in both halves but involve Uranus, Neptune or Pluto, which are outside the Vedic scope and cannot become rules.
