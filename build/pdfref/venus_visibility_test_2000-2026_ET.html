The January 2026 case

Venus rose in the West on Sat 31 Jan 2026 (1 Feb IST), in Capricorn / Dhanishta with Sun, Mars – so the qualified form of the rule applies (sharp upturn after a small dip). Sky at noon ET on 31 Jan: Sun Capricorn Shravana; Moon Cancer Punarvasu; Mercury Capricorn Dhanishta; Venus Capricorn Dhanishta; Mars Capricorn Shravana; Jupiter Gemini Punarvasu R; Saturn Pisces Uttara Bhadrapada; Rahu Aquarius Shatabhisha R; Ketu Leo Purva Phalguni R.

Gold fell 10.8% on Fri 30 Jan and 1.95% on Mon 2 Feb, then rose 6.1% on Tue 3 Feb and was back above 5,000 by 9 Feb. The fall came the session before the date the layer gives for the rising, so under the standard scoring (entry 2026-01-30 at 4,745.1) the event is credited with the move after the crash: 5 sessions +4.95%, 20 sessions +11.94%, 40 sessions -4.62%; the 3 sessions into the event -6.64%. Read loosely – a fall around the rising followed by a sharp recovery – it matches the qualified rule's “dip then sharp upturn”, but the dip was not small and it came first.
