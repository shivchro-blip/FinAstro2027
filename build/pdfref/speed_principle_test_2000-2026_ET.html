"Slow planets set the trend, fast planets the sudden moves": test on gold and silver, 2000–2026

Idea as stated in Kindle Ch.3 / Ch.6 (Moon intraday, slow planets the primary trend), TA p.59–60 (Moon very short term; Mercury a sudden sentiment changer) and KD p.82 / p.129 (slow motion prolongs a move). COMEX GC=F and SI=F daily closes, Aug 2000 – Nov 2026; events from the astronomy layer (Swiss Ephemeris, Lahiri). Gold result: after fast-planet events the next-5-day move averaged ×1.03 of a normal 5-day move (slow-planet events ×0.94); significantly bigger moves after: none. Slow-planet-only rules hit 57.1% (base 51.7%), fast-planet-only rules 54.6% (base 53.1%).
