Does the silver signal table's High / Moderate / Low hold up? Backtest 2000–2026

The table's scoring (tools/gold_signal_table.py) applied to every past trading day; consecutive days with the same signal and strength form one episode, scored like a rule (entry = last close before; 3+ sessions to the last close, otherwise 5 sessions; base = same horizon, same Nov–Nov year). If the strengths mean something, High should beat Moderate and Moderate should beat Low, and all should beat their base.
