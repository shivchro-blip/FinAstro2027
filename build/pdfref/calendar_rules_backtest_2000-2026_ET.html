The 2027 calendar's own rules (2026 document: catalogue B, configuration groups, sub-tables), backtested 2000–2026

The calendar layer (finastro/verdicts.py) was generated for every year 2000–2026 exactly as for 2027 and scored on the instruments it names: SPX rows on the S&P 500, NIFTY rows on the BSE Sensex (26-year proxy), GC on COMEX gold, SI on COMEX silver. Rows with a printed direction are scored on direction (same method as every other backtest); rows printed with no direction — Bhadra, Panchak, Amavasya/Purnima, eclipses, turning dates and the like — are scored on whether a big day (±2%, silver ±3.5%) fell inside the episode, against the chance of one over the same number of sessions in that year.
