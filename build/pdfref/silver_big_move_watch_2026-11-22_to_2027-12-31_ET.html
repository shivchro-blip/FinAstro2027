On a normal day the chance of a ±3.5% close was 8.4%; the last column of the first table gives the historical rate when each lead was present (not adjusted for year, so it flatters factors active in volatile years).

How much to trust it. No lead passed the false-discovery-rate correction (lowest q 0.18). When leads were picked on 2000–2013 only, 3 of 17 stayed above 1 on 2013–2026, but taken together they gave 330 big days against 363.7 expected (×0.91): no edge out of sample. Read the dates below as times to expect volatility to be possible, not as calls.
