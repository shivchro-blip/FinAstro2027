Bhadra — verified against Swiss Ephemeris

↑ Back to Table of Contents
Method. Every window is computed with the Swiss Ephemeris. A Bhadra (Vishti karana) is a 6° step of Moon−Sun elongation, so the eight windows of each lunar month fall on karana numbers 8, 15, 22, 29, 36, 43, 50 and 57. All times are IST (Mumbai).

Stay (Day / Night Bhadra) is karana parity — odd (first half of the tithi) = Day Bhadra, even = Night Bhadra; it never changes with place or timezone.

Sign is the Moon’s sidereal sign (Lahiri) when the window opens; Bhadra Nature 2 follows the loka rule — Aries/Taurus/Gemini/Scorpio → Swarg, Virgo/Libra/Sagittarius/Capricorn → Patal, Cancer/Leo/Aquarius/Pisces → Earth (Mrityu-loka), the malefic case.

Bhadra Nature 1 asks whether the window opens after sunrise (Day Time) or after sunset (Night Time) at Mumbai; this is the one local column. Result 1: a Bhadra opening in the opposite period is defused (Positive), in the same period it is live (Negative). Overall is Positive or Negative when both results agree, Neutral when they split.
