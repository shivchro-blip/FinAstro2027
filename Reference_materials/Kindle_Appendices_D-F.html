Appendix D Consolidated Glossary

D.1 Astrological & Jyotish terms

Antardasha
a sub-period within a mahadasha; the second level of the Vimshottari timeline (Ch 12).
Arudha pada
in Jaimini, a sign's "image" or perceived manifestation, derived by a counting rule (Ch 0, library Cluster D).
Ascendant (Lagna)
the zodiac degree rising on the eastern horizon at a given moment; anchors the house framework. Time-sensitive and therefore fragile for market charts (Ch 13).
Aspect (drishti)
an angular relationship between bodies. Western aspects are symmetrical (conjunction, sextile, square, trine, opposition); Vedic drishti are directional and special by planet (Ch 0, App B.2). Aspect angles are zodiac-independent (Ch 2).
Ayanamsa
the angular gap between the tropical and sidereal zodiacs (~24.23° Lahiri today), growing ~1°/72 yr by precession. Lahiri and KP ayanamsas differ by ~6′ (Ch 2).
Bhadra
the interval during which Vishti karana runs, interchangeable with it in almanac usage; a prohibition on beginnings. Unrelated to the nakshatras Purva and Uttara Bhadrapada despite the shared syllable (Ch 6A).
Bhadra-vaas
Bhadra's residence — which of the three worlds it occupies during a window, read from the Moon's sidereal rashi (Ch 6A).
Bhava
a house; one of twelve life-arenas measured from the ascendant. The "wealth houses" (2, 11; also 5, 9) and "loss houses" (8, 12) recur in market work (Ch 5).
Combustion (asta)
a planet too close to the Sun to act freely; orbs in App B.6 (Ch 7).
Dasha
a planetary period system timing when influences activate. Vimshottari (120-yr, Moon-nakshatra-based) is the default (Ch 12).
Day Bhadra / Night Bhadra
classification of a Bhadra window by karana parity — odd gives Day, even gives Night; equivalently the first versus the second half of a tithi. A property of the lunar month, not of daylight, and invariant with timezone (Ch 6A).
Debilitation (neecha)
a planet in its sign of greatest weakness (App B.1).
Dignity
a planet's strength-by-placement: exaltation, own sign, moolatrikona, debilitation (Ch 7, App B.1).
Divisional chart (varga)
a harmonic subdivision of the zodiac for fine analysis (e.g., D-9 Navamsa, D-12 Dwadasamsa) (Ch 0, library).
Exaltation (uccha)
a planet's sign of greatest strength (App B.1).
Gochar (transit)
the current sky read against a natal chart, classically from the natal Moon (Ch 12).
Graha
a "planet" of Jyotish: Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, plus the lunar nodes Rahu and Ketu (Ch 0).
Karana
a half-tithi: 6° of Moon–Sun elongation, sixty to a lunar month; the fifth limb of the Panchanga (Ch 6, 6A).
KP (Krishnamurti Paddhati)
a stellar system refining placement to sign → star (nakshatra) → sub-lord, using the KP ayanamsa and Placidus houses; the most precise and falsifiable school (Ch 14).
Lal Kitab
a distinct North-Indian remedial system with its own rules (library Cluster G).
Mahadasha
a major planetary period (the top level of Vimshottari) (Ch 12).
Moolatrikona
a planet's "root-trine" zone of strong functional dignity (App B.1).
Mrityu-loka
the earthly residence of Bhadra; Moon in Cancer, Leo, Aquarius or Pisces. The only case the tradition treats as genuinely malefic (Ch 6A).
Muhurta
electional timing — the choosing of an auspicious moment, assembled from the Panchanga (Ch 6, 6A).
Nadi astrology
predictive lineages (Bhrigu, Suka, Chandra Kala/Deva Keralam) (library Cluster C).
Nakshatra
one of 27 lunar mansions of 13°20′ each, each with a ruling planet; the Moon's nakshatra seeds the Vimshottari dasha (Ch 0, App B.3).
Pada
a quarter (3°20′) of a nakshatra (App A.4).
Paksha
a fortnight of fifteen tithis: Shukla (bright, waxing) or Krishna (dark, waning) (Ch 6).
Panchanga
the Hindu almanac's five limbs: tithi, vara (weekday), nakshatra, yoga and karana (Ch 6, 6A).
Patala-loka
the nether residence of Bhadra; Moon in Virgo, Libra, Sagittarius or Capricorn. Held harmless to earthly affairs (Ch 6A).
Prashna (horary)
answering a question from the chart of the moment it is asked (Ch 14, library Cluster H).
Retrograde (vakri)
apparent backward motion (negative longitude-speed) (Ch 7).
Sade Sati
the ~7.5-yr period of Saturn transiting the 12th, 1st, and 2nd from the natal Moon (Ch 12).
Shadbala
the classical six-source composite of planetary strength (Ch 7).
Significator
the body standing for a given matter or market force; resolved via a chain of rulerships (Ch 4).
Station
the moment a planet's speed crosses zero as it turns retrograde or direct; a tradeable state in the book's framing (Ch 7, 10).
Sub-lord
in KP, the planet ruling the sub-division of a star that contains a point; "the sub decides" (Ch 14).
Swarga-loka
the heavenly residence of Bhadra; Moon in Aries, Taurus, Gemini or Scorpio. Held harmless to earthly affairs (Ch 6A).
Tithi
a lunar "day," one of 30 Sun–Moon angular steps of 12°; zodiac-independent (Ch 6, App A.6).
Vishti
the seventh and last of the seven movable karanas; the only one carrying a prohibition rather than a flavour. Falls on karanas 8, 15, 22, 29, 36, 43, 50 and 57 of every lunar month (Ch 6A).
Void-of-course Moon
the Moon making no further major aspect before changing sign; a "low-conviction" window (Ch 6).
Yoga
a defined planetary combination (e.g., Dhana yoga for wealth) (Ch 5).

D.2 Western-cycle, market, statistical & risk terms

Anomaly decay
the tendency of a documented market edge to weaken after publication (McLean & Pontiff, 2016) (Ch 19).
Base rate
how often an event occurs anyway; the benchmark any "predictor" must beat (Ch 18).
Bradley siderograph
Donald Bradley's weighted aspect-sum curve projecting turn dates (not levels); zodiac-independent (Ch 11).
Confusion matrix
the 2×2 of hits, misses, false alarms, and correct passes used to score a rule honestly (Ch 18).
Data-mining / multiple comparisons / look-elsewhere
finding a "signal" by testing many rules; guarantees an impressive in-sample winner by chance (Ch 18).
Drawdown
a peak-to-trough capital loss; recovery is asymmetric (−50% needs +100%) (Ch 20).
Efficient markets
the null hypothesis that prices already reflect available information, so simple public edges get arbitraged away (Ch 19).
FII / DII
Foreign / Domestic Institutional Investors; major flow drivers in Indian markets (Ch 3).
Fixed-fractional sizing
risking a small constant fraction of capital per trade (e.g., 1–2%) (Ch 20).
Grand Mutation / trigon
the shift of Jupiter–Saturn conjunctions into a new element (~200-yr); the 2020 conjunction began an Air series (a tropical statement) (Ch 8).
Halloween effect ("Sell in May")
the seasonal pattern of higher Nov–Apr returns (Bouman & Jacobsen, 2002); robust-ish but contested (Ch 19).
In-sample / out-of-sample
data used to develop a rule vs. untouched data used to test it; only the latter is evidence (Ch 18).
Ingress
a planet's entry into a new sign; a datable trigger (Ch 10).
Kelly criterion
the bet fraction maximizing long-run log-growth given a real edge; over-betting past it destroys capital (Ch 20).
Lunar effect
slightly higher returns around the new vs. full moon (Dichev & Janes 2003; Yuan et al. 2006); small, behavioral, partly-unreplicated (Ch 19).
McWhirter Theory
Louise McWhirter's 1937 nodal business-cycle model (low at Aquarius node, high at Leo); uses the tropical node (Ch 9).
Pre-registration
committing a rule and its hit criterion before looking at outcomes (Ch 18).
Risk of ruin
the probability of catastrophic capital loss; driven more by bet size than by signal (Ch 20).
STT
Securities Transaction Tax; an India-specific trading cost that erodes marginal edges (Ch 18, 20).
Survivorship bias
remembering "hits" (and surviving stocks/funds) while ignoring the rest (Ch 18).
Volatility drag
the compounding penalty by which large swings lower median wealth even on a fair game (Ch 20).

Appendix F NotebookLM Query Pack — for the 78-Book Library

Paste these into NotebookLM (or a similar AI tool with your library loaded) to mine the wider collection. They are written to extract verifiable rules, surface disagreements, and keep the honest, testable stance of this book. Replace bracketed terms as needed.

F.1 Universal extraction prompts (any text)

"List every concrete, testable rule in [BOOK] for timing market or event turns. For each, quote the rule in under 15 words, give the page, and label it deterministic, conditional, or vague."
"Summarize [BOOK]'s core method in 200 words, then list its key assumptions and any claims it presents without evidence."
"Extract every numeric parameter in [BOOK] (orbs, degrees, periods, thresholds) into a table with page references."
"What does [BOOK] claim about [topic, e.g., the 8th house / Saturn transits / eclipses]? Quote sparingly and cite pages."

F.2 Cross-source synthesis (multiple texts)

"Compare how [BOOK A], [BOOK B], and [BOOK C] define and use [significator / sub-lord / arudha]. Build a table of agreements and disagreements with citations."
"Across all KP sources in the library, list every rule for identifying a market turn via the cuspal sub-lord. Flag where sources conflict."
"Find every claim across the library that the Moon (or Mercury retrograde) affects short-term price/sentiment. Note which give a falsifiable rule vs. a general statement."
"Where do the Parashari and Jaimini sources in the library give contradictory instructions for the same judgement? List with citations."

F.3 Honest-stance / critical prompts

"For [BOOK]'s central predictive claim, design a pre-registered, out-of-sample test: state the signal, the prediction, the dated window, the hit criterion, and the base rate to beat."
"Identify any 'crash signature' or 'sure-fire' claim in the library and list the misses or counter-examples a skeptic would raise."
"Which claims in [BOOK] are unfalsifiable (always 'in some period', or tunable after the fact)? Explain why for each."
"Separate [BOOK]'s claims into zodiac-independent (aspects, cycles, tithi) and zodiac-dependent (sign, house, nakshatra, sub-lord). Which would change under a different ayanamsa?"

F.4 Financial-astrology cluster prompts

"From the financial-astrology sources (Williams, Meridian, Weingarten, McWhirter, Bradley, Lee & Tryde), extract every distinct market-timing technique into one master table: name, inputs, output (date/level/direction), and source."
"Reconcile the tropical basis of the Western financial-astrology sources with the sidereal basis of the Indian sources for the same date. Where would their readings of [a chosen date] diverge, and where would they agree?"
"List every commodity or sector rulership asserted anywhere in the library. Mark which are widely shared vs. idiosyncratic to one author."

F.5 Study-and-build prompts

"Generate 20 exam-style questions (with answers and citations) that test mastery of [cluster, e.g., Nadi / KP / dasha systems] from the library."
"Draft a one-page 'rules cheat-sheet' for [topic] citing only the library, marking each rule [Classical], [disputed], or [single-source]."
"Build a timeline of the historical development of [KP / Nadi / Jaimini] using only datable facts from the library; flag undated claims."
